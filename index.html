Federal Hemp Ban — Nov 12 Deadline

Aug 9 🔴 Key Vote Verified — Senate.gov

Senate Votes 61–32 to Keep Hemp Delay in Funding Bill

The Senate tabled Sen. Budd's amendment (which would have kept Nov 12 intact) 61–32, then passed the full Continuing Resolution H.R. 6500 90–6 — with a rider moving the hemp THC product ban from Nov 12 to Dec 11, 2026 for naturally-derived products. Verified against Senate.gov roll calls 227 & 228.

Aug 9 ⚠️ Critical

Synthetics Carve-Out: Nov 12 is Permanent — No Delay Possible

Even if the House concurs on the CR delay, synthetic and converted cannabinoids (those "not capable of being naturally produced by a Cannabis sativa L. plant") remain permanently banned Nov 12 — no extension, no override. This is in the statute itself.

Aug 8 Policy Signal

Hemp Fight Threatens Government Shutdown — Shutdown ≠ Extension

A government shutdown would NOT toll or extend the Nov 12 statutory deadline. A lapse in appropriations halts non-excepted spending but does NOT affect substantive statutory effective dates. A shutdown would actually lock in Nov 12 by blocking passage of the CR delay.

Aug 7 Policy Verified — Marijuana Moment

Trump White House Supports Hemp Delay in CR

WH spokesperson formally stated support for the CR provision delaying the hemp ban. WH legislative affairs committed to "no further extensions" — Dec 11 is the ceiling if the delay passes. The "Susie Wiles family" allegation (Bret Worley / MC Nutraceuticals) is an ethics/political story with no bearing on the statutory outcome.

Aug 10 New Bill

Beverage Regulatory Parity Act — Hemp Drinks Only

Rep. Van Duyne (R-TX) and Rep. Landsman (D-OH) introduced a bipartisan bill to regulate hemp THC beverages like alcohol (5mg/serving, TTB regulation). Backed by WSWA and BAMCO (alcohol trade groups). Covers beverages only — explicitly does NOT cover gummies or edibles. No near-term floor action expected.

Aug 5 Expert Commentary

CLN Attorney: Congress Won't Save Hemp Before Nov 12

Cannabis Legalization News attorney (cannabis law practitioner) categorically stated Congressional rescue is unlikely before Nov 12. Consistent with H.R. 9830 (no cosponsors, no committee action) and WH's "no further extensions" commitment. This was the prediction before the Senate vote — the Senate delay now provides a Dec 11 ceiling if the House acts.

Rescheduling & 280E Tax Relief

Aug 7 Earnings Signal

Cresco Labs Books $26.7M 280E Tax Benefit in Q2

Cresco Labs reported a $26.7M 280E tax benefit in Q2 2026 ($73M revenue, $40M adj. EBITDA), suggesting operators are booking tax relief under an accrual theory pending the final rescheduling rule. Caveat: 280E final rule is NOT yet in effect — DEA ALJ process is still ongoing; Aug 17 was the briefing deadline, with recommendation expected Sept–Oct 2026. Cresco files on SEDAR+ not SEC; figures from TDR/RSS, not independently verified from primary press release.

Aug 2 Rescheduling Watch Verified — Federal Register

Bill Barr & SAM Slam Rescheduling — Process Still Contested

Former AG Bill Barr and SAM CEO Kevin Sabet published a WaPo op-ed opposing rescheduling (July 29). SAM is a designated hearing party in the DEA ALJ proceeding. The rescheduling process remains: DEA ALJ hearing concluded ~July 15; recommendation due in 60–90 days; then final rule. Acting AG Blanche's interim order (Apr 23) moved state-licensed medical MJ to Schedule III immediately — broader rescheduling still pending. 280E is still in effect for now.

Texas & State Hemp Bans

Aug 3 Active Law Verified — DSHS

Texas Hemp Ban Active Since July 31 — $500M Market Gone

Texas DSHS Schedule I scheduling action (enabled by TX Supreme Court immunity reversal in Sky Marketing No. 23-0887) took effect July 31. Delta-8, delta-10, THCP, and category "tetrahydrocannabinols" from hemp are now Schedule I in Texas. Fine up to $25,000/violation. A new federal lawsuit was filed Aug 1 in S.D. Tex. (2 retailers + 1 distributor; lead counsel Andrea Steel) seeking TRO+PI on Farm Bill preemption grounds — outcome pending.

Aug 6 Political Advocacy

TX Senate Candidate Talarico Calls Ban "Insane" / "Gift to Cartels"

James Talarico (TX State Rep, 2026 Democratic Senate nominee — beat Crockett 52.4–46.2%) is continuing his opposition messaging on SB 3. Both quotes ("insane" = May 2025 floor speech; "gift to the cartels" = Aug 3, 2026 statement) are real but political advocacy, not a regulatory development.

Dr. Oz / Medicare Hemp Signal

Aug 10–11 Policy Split Verified — Marijuana Moment

Dr. Oz Enters Hemp Fight — CBD for Seniors, Not THC Edibles

CMS Administrator Dr. Oz sent a formal letter asking senators to oppose Budd's strip amendment — to protect a Medicare hemp-CBD coverage initiative ($500/yr, ≤3mg THC/serving for seniors via ACO Reach/Oncology/LEAD models). This is the policy split: the administration is defending therapeutic hemp-CBD for seniors; it is NOT defending "intoxicating edibles at gas stations" (per Budd's framing, which Oz did not rebut).

Macro & Social Signals

Aug 4 Federal Data Verified — SAMHSA 2025 NSDUH

Cannabis Now #1 Daily-Use Substance in America — Federal Data

SAMHSA's 2025 National Survey on Drug Use and Health: 21.4M daily/near-daily cannabis users vs. 17.2M alcohol and 19.9M cigarette smokers. Cannabis is now the most commonly used daily substance in the US per federal data. Note: "daily/near-daily" = 20+ days in 30; cigarettes use a stricter every-day threshold — comparison is accurate but has a methodological caveat.
