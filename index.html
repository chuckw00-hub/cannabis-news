Legislation — What's Real vs. What Isn't

Aug 13 NOT Real Legislation Verified — GovTrack + PDF Metadata

"Full-Spectrum CBD and Hemp Clarification Act of 2026" — It's a Lawyer's Draft, Not a Bill

CLN (Cannabis Legalization News) published a video titled "The CBD Bill Nobody Is Talking About" covering this proposal. It is not a bill before Congress. Verified via GovTrack (0 results), GovInfo, and the PDF itself — metadata confirms: Author = Thomas E. Howard (cannabis attorney / CLN host), Subject = "Prepared for legislative consideration; not introduced legislation," Creator = "Cannabis Industry Lawyer outreach package."

What the draft proposes: amend the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 to protect full-spectrum CBD, while explicitly excluding intoxicating and synthetic hemp-derived products. If something like this ever became law, intoxicating hemp-derived THC products would fall in the "intoxicating" exclusion — the draft offers them no protection.

Vermont — CCB & Local Updates

CCB Adopts Updated Product Registration Guidance — Review Now

The Vermont CCB Board adopted updated Product Registration Guidance at the July 29, 2026 Board meeting. Updates include clarifications to extraction terminology, font expectations on product labels, and other technical revisions. The updated guidance is live now at ccb.vermont.gov/guidance.

Jul 29 CCB Board — Comment by Aug 28 ⏰ Comment Deadline Aug 28 Official — ccb.vermont.gov

CCB Draft Advertising Guidance — 4 Material Changes, Comment Window Open

The CCB posted a draft updated Advertising Guidance for 30-day public comment (~deadline Aug 28, 2026). Four material changes:

  • 🆕 AI-Assisted Advertising (NEW RULE): First-ever AI content subsection — no misleading product images, no modified warning symbols, exact health warning text required. Any AI-assisted image workflow needs checking against these three requirements.
  • Age-Gating (Clarification): Age gate must fire on direct sub-page entry, not just homepage — a technical change for most licensee sites.
  • Social Media Health Warning (Update): Pinned comment must be visible across all devices and platforms — stricter than the Feb 2026 wording. May affect Instagram/Facebook pinning compliance.
  • Prize/Loyalty Promotions (Already in effect since Jun 2024): Codifies Act 166 of 2023 — spin-a-wheel prizes at checkout and loyalty programs are now permitted for adult-use retail, with pre-approval.
Ongoing Vermont Context

Vermont Already Stricter Than Federal

Vermont adopted its own intoxicating hemp ban with a 10mg/package cap effective July 1, 2026. Delta-8, delta-9-from-hemp, and similar manufactured cannabinoids already require Vermont licensing — unlicensed hemp THC products are already non-compliant in Vermont. Licensed Vermont operators are already working under that state framework. The Nov 12 federal deadline adds a federal layer on top of what Vermont has already done.
